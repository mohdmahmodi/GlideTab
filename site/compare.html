What to look for in a window switcher

The switcher built into every Mac, ⌘Tab, moves between apps, not windows. If you keep several windows of the same app open, it can't take you to a specific one. Every app on this page fixes that in its own way: a grid of previews, a list, a sidebar, or a search field.

The things that separate them are whether you can see what each window looks like before you pick it, whether you can type a name instead of tapping through the list, whether browser tabs count as windows, which versions of macOS they run on, and what they cost. GlideTab is free and open source, shows live previews, searches as you type, and includes browser tabs. It needs macOS 14 or later.
